Yes, these metrics can typically be computed over various time periods such as daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly, depending on the specific needs and the available data. Here’s how these periods are often used:

### Daily

1. **Total Likes, Shares, Comments**: Calculated daily to track the day-to-day engagement on posts.
2. **Daily Reach and Impressions**: To understand daily audience engagement and content visibility.
3. **Daily Average Engagement Rate**: To monitor how daily interactions compare to daily reach.
4. **Daily Growth Rates**: To observe short-term trends and spikes in engagement, reach, or followers.

### Weekly

1. **Weekly Totals (Likes, Shares, Comments, etc.)**: Aggregated weekly to see the performance over a longer period.
2. **Weekly Average Metrics**: Calculating weekly averages to smooth out daily fluctuations.
3. **Weekly Growth Rates**: To identify medium-term trends and assess the effectiveness of weekly campaigns.
4. **Weekly Engagement Trends**: To spot patterns and changes in user interaction on a week-to-week basis.

### Monthly

1. **Monthly Totals**: Comprehensive totals for likes, shares, comments, reach, and impressions over a month.
2. **Monthly Average Engagement Rate**: Provides a broader view of engagement relative to reach over a month.
3. **Monthly Growth Rates**: For assessing long-term growth and the success of monthly strategies.
4. **Monthly Trends**: To identify seasonal trends and longer-term changes in user behavior.

### Yearly

1. **Yearly Totals**: Aggregated data for the entire year to evaluate overall performance.
2. **Yearly Averages**: For understanding average engagement, reach, and impressions on an annual basis.
3. **Yearly Growth Rates**: To analyze growth over the long term and compare year-over-year performance.
4. **Yearly Trends**: To observe significant shifts and patterns over the years, helping in strategic planning.

These time frames allow for both short-term and long-term analysis, providing insights that can be used to refine content strategies, improve user engagement, and achieve marketing goals.
